About

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group. He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.
